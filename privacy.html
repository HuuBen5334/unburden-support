Unburden

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Unburden is designed to be a private journaling app. Your writing stays on your device.

Data we collect

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. Unburden has no account system, analytics, advertising, tracking, cloud backup service, or server API.

Your journal

Journal entries, check-ins, and app preferences are stored locally on your device. If you choose to release an entry, its written text is deleted from the app; only non-identifying check-in numbers used for Insights may remain.

Optional on-device features

If you turn on the optional follow-up-question feature, Unburden uses Apple’s on-device language model to process the text on your device. Your entry text is not sent to Unburden, to us, or to another service. You can leave this feature off at any time.

Face ID and notifications

If enabled, Face ID is used only to unlock the app on your device. If you enable reminders, notifications do not include your journal entry content.

Your choices

You can delete individual entries or use “Delete everything” in the app to remove all locally stored entries and check-ins. Deleting the app may also remove locally stored app data, subject to your device settings and backups.

Children

Unburden is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, please visit the Unburden support page. Please do not include journal entry text in a support request.
