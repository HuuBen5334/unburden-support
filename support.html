Unburden is a private, on-device journaling app. We’re here to help with the app itself.

Request support on GitHub

Before you write

Please do not include your journal entry text, passwords, or other sensitive personal information in a support request. We do not need it to help troubleshoot the app.

Common questions

Where is my journal data stored?
Your entries and check-ins stay on your device. Unburden does not provide an account or cloud-sync service.

How do I delete my data?
In Unburden, open the You tab and choose “Delete everything.” You can also delete individual kept entries from Journal.

What if I need immediate help?
Unburden is a complement to care, not a replacement for it. In the United States, call or text 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. Elsewhere, contact local emergency services or a local crisis-support service.
